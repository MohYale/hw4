# Design

## What was requested

Add photographs of Yale University to this product website:

- Harkness Tower
- Cross Campus
- Evans Hall
- the main campus
- the medical school
- Payne Whitney Gym

Handsome Dan should appear on the page and walk around the screen while a customer shops.

This matters because it makes the website aesthetically pleasing, it will bring memories for the graduate customer, and it will remind the currently matriculating customer about the exclusivity of Yale University.

## What was added

The home page and the about page show those six places in a gallery, each photograph labeled with the place.

Handsome Dan is on every page, including the catalogue and a product page. His portrait moves around the edges of the screen while the shopper browses. He does not block clicks, and the chat button stays in front of him. If the browser asks for less motion, he stays still in the lower left.
